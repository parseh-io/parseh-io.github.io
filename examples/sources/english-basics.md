:::exercise fill-blanks
prompt: Complete the sentence.
text: She [[verb]] to school every [[when]].
- [verb] walks
- [when] morning
- [ ] walk
- [ ] yesterday
explanation-correct: *She* takes the **-s** form, *walks*, and *every* asks for a time that repeats: *every morning*.
:::
