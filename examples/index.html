Examples

Pages made with Parseh and exported from it as they are: each is one file that works in any browser, with nothing behind it. They are a small part of Parseh; nothing you answer here is kept.

  • English: one exercise of every kindFifteen small exercises for a learner of English, one of each kind Parseh has: blanks to fill, sentences to order and to build, three ways of matching, six of choosing, and three flashcards. Some use pictograms.Open the deck
  • Mathematics and chemistryLimits, an integral, derivatives, a proof to put in order, series, reactions to balance and molecules to name: formulas drawn by LaTeX inside the exercises, in the blanks and the answers too.Open the deck
